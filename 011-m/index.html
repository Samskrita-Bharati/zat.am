How to play

  1. Start Press आरम्भ to reveal a Sanskrit month.
  2. Find its position Count clockwise from १. चैत्रः is the first month.
  3. Place the month Drag the displayed month into its correct slot, or tap the slot. An incorrect placement adds one wrong attempt.
  4. Complete the cycle Press the next-month button to reveal another month. You can also use it to skip the displayed month. Place all twelve months to finish.

Keyboard controls
Press Enter to start. Use the left and right arrow keys to select a slot, then press Enter to place the month. After a correct placement, press Enter to reveal another month. To skip, use Tab to focus the next-month button and press Enter.

Scoring
Fewer wrong attempts give a higher final score. Place all twelve months without mistakes to earn 100. Your score is submitted only after completing the game.

उत्तमम्! You won!

You placed all twelve months in the correct order.
