How to play

  1. Start Press Start to reveal a Sanskrit month.
  2. Find its position Count clockwise from the gold position marked १. चैत्रः is the first month.
  3. Drag and place Drag the month into its correct position. Then press Next month.
  4. Complete the cycle Place all twelve months to finish.

Use the left and right arrow keys to rotate the ring.

Fewer wrong attempts give a higher final score. Twelve correct placements with no mistakes earn 100.
