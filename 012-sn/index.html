How to play

  1. Start Press आरम्भ to reveal a Surya Namaskar invocation.
  2. Find its position Count clockwise from १. ॐ मित्राय नमः is the first invocation.
  3. Place the invocation Drag the displayed invocation into its correct slot, or tap the slot. An incorrect placement adds one wrong attempt.
  4. Complete the cycle The next invocation appears automatically after a correct placement. Use अग्रिमम् to skip the displayed invocation. Place all twelve invocations to finish.

Keyboard controls
Press Enter to start. Use the left and right arrow keys to select a slot, then press Enter to place the invocation. After a correct placement, the next invocation appears automatically. To skip, use Tab to focus अग्रिमम् and press Enter.

Scoring
Fewer wrong attempts give a higher final score. Place all twelve invocations without mistakes to earn 100. Your score is submitted only after completing the game.

उत्तमम्! You won!

You placed all twelve invocations in the correct order.
