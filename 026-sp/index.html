How to play

  1. Select Start.
  2. Press on the red vowel and drag to paint it.
  3. Lift your finger or release the mouse between separate strokes.
  4. Keep the centre of the brush inside the letter. Going outside gives you another try.
  5. After painting enough of the vowel, release to complete it.

Try again repeats the same vowel. Next letter and New letter choose a random vowel.

Use Sound on/off to control feedback sounds.

The timer starts with your first stroke and continues through retries. Selecting a vowel or New letter starts a fresh attempt.

Painted percentage updates when you release a stroke. Thin unpainted edges may still be accepted for completion.

Select a vowel to see its saved personal best. Green checks and best scores remain after reloading this browser. An unfinished drawing starts fresh after reload.

Based on Letterpaint by Christian Heilmann.
