How to play

  1. Press Scramble to mix the tiles.
  2. Click a numbered tile next to the empty space to slide it.
  3. Arrange the numbers from left to right, starting at the top.
  4. Leave the empty space in the bottom-right corner.

Hover over a tile to see its number in English digits.

The timer starts after scrambling. Time and valid moves both affect your score. Your final score out of 100, moves, and time appear when you complete the puzzle.

Solve displays the completed arrangement and ends the attempt without awarding points.

Sign in to Zat.am to save a completed game’s score to the leaderboard.
