# 003-ssc — सोपानसर्पाः

A Sanskrit Snakes and Ladders game for Zat.am.

The player competes against the computer, rolls the dice, moves across a 100-square board, climbs ladders, slides down snakes, and learns Sanskrit numbers during gameplay.

---

## How to Play

1. The game randomly selects who starts first.
2. When it is your turn, click the dice to roll.
3. The dice generates a number from 1 to 6.
4. Your piece moves forward by the rolled amount.
5. Landing on a ladder moves the player upward.
6. Landing on a snake moves the player downward.
7. The computer plays automatically on its turn.
8. The first player to reach square 100 wins.

---

## Sanskrit Learning

The board displays:

- Standard numbers
- Devanagari numbers
- Sanskrit number forms

When a player reaches a square, the game can also play the corresponding number audio.

This allows the game to combine Snakes and Ladders gameplay with Sanskrit number practice.
